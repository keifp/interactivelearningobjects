Virtual field trips & branching scenarios

Two formats that do the most work in online courses: putting learners somewhere they can't easily go, and putting them in a decision they have to own.

Virtual field trips

A real place, rebuilt in the browser, that learners walk through at their own pace. Hotspots hold the expert commentary you'd give on a site visit, a checklist tracks what they've inspected, and it all runs without an app or a headset.

  • Clinical and home-care environments, labs, worksites, historic sites, and facilities
  • Built from photogrammetry, 3D models, or 360° photo captures
  • Optional notes panel and completion tracking for graded use
Walk through the sample ↗

Branching scenarios

A story that changes based on what the learner decides. Each choice leads somewhere plausible, consequences accumulate, and a debrief at the end connects the path they took back to the concepts in the course.

  • Ethics, communication, policy, safety, and professional-judgment topics
  • Written with you from real cases; multiple endings and a "your path" summary
  • Reports completion or a score to the LMS when packaged as SCORM

How a project works

Most objects go from first call to delivery in one to three weeks, depending on scope.

01 — CONSULTA free 30-minute call to understand the learning goal, your content, and where the object will live.
02 — PROTOTYPEA working draft with placeholder content so you can react to the interaction before the details are locked.
03 — BUILDYour content, media, and feedback go in. Accessibility and mobile behavior are checked along the way.
04 — DELIVERFinal files as embed code, a hosted link, or a SCORM package, plus a short guide for updating it later.
