Animated Step Pathway

Progression From Referral To Placement

The pathway below visually moves through the four major stages of the special education process.

Step 1

Referral

Concern raised by a parent or school staff member.

The team first attempts instructional modifications before formal evaluation.

Step 2

Assessment

A multidisciplinary team evaluates the student using multiple data sources:

  • Cognitive testing
  • Academic assessments
  • Behavioral observations
  • Interviews and records review
Step 3

Development of an IEP

The Individualized Education Program includes:

  • Present performance levels
  • Measurable goals
  • Accommodations and services
Step 4

Placement in the Least Restrictive Environment

Students should be educated with peers without disabilities whenever appropriate.
