The sequence

From First Concern to Classroom Placement

These four stages happen in order. Each one depends on what the previous stage produced.

Stage 1

Referral

Someone, usually a parent or a member of the school staff, flags a concern about the student.

Before any formal evaluation, the team tries adjusting instruction to see whether that resolves the issue.

Stage 2

Assessment

A team drawn from several disciplines studies the student through more than one kind of evidence:

  • Tests of cognitive ability
  • Measures of academic skill
  • Observation of behavior
  • Interviews and a review of existing records
Stage 3

Writing the IEP

The Individualized Education Program sets out:

  • Where the student's performance stands today
  • Goals that can actually be measured
  • The accommodations and services to be provided
Stage 4

Placement in the Least Restrictive Environment

Wherever it is appropriate, the student learns alongside peers who do not have disabilities.
