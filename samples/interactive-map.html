State Housing & Health Agencies

Role in the System

Texas-level agencies (TDHCA, HHSC) administering housing tax credits, behavioral-health funding, and health-coverage decisions that shape local capacity.

What They Control

Low-Income Housing Tax Credit allocations, state mental-health block grants, Medicaid policy, and emergency funding releases.

Primary Incentives

Legislative priorities, federal pass-through compliance, gubernatorial direction, and budget cycles.

Equity Considerations

State decisions about Medicaid expansion and behavioral-health coverage have outsized effects on which communities can sustain services.

Understanding Homelessness as a Local System

In Waco, homelessness is shaped by interactions across housing markets, healthcare systems, public safety, and community priorities — often unevenly across neighborhoods. Leaders act most responsibly when they understand how choices in one place reverberate across the whole system.
